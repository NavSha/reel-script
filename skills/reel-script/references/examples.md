# Worked example: 070 (approved)

An approved script with notes on why each line is there. Channel: @build.with.navneet
(Indian audience, ~25-year-old aspiring PMs and founders). Approved 19 Nov 2026.

## The script, line by line

> An actor's side quest just became one of the biggest wins in AI history.

Hook. The reviewer wrote this line. It hides the name and promises a big story.

> In 2023, he bet 435 million dollars on an AI company nobody was talking about. Today, that bet is worth seven and a half billion.

The proof, with two numbers spoken in words. The viewer gets the scale without doing maths.

> Yes, Ashton Kutcher. The guy from That '70s Show.

The reveal comes on line 3. A five-word "who he is" helps viewers who don't know the name.

> The company is Anthropic. The one that makes Claude. He and his partners got in back in 2023, when it was a small startup. Today, it went public at one and a half trillion dollars, the biggest stock market launch in history.

Name + "what it is" in five words. "He and his partners" is honest: a fund with outside
investors holds the stake. "Fund" was cut as jargon. Third number, the last one.

> And he didn't try to pick one winner. He backed OpenAI and Anthropic at the same time. In his words: "every company is going to be an AI company."

The one core idea: bet on the shift, not on a single winner. A real quote, with its source in the notes.

> Think of it like buying land near a metro line before it opens. You don't know which shop on that street will do best. You just know the crowd is coming.

One analogy from Indian daily life, in three sentences.

> You can't buy Anthropic at 2023 prices. But you can make the same bet with your career. Bet on the shift. Whatever your job is, learn to do it with AI.

It turns the story into something for the viewer. "You can't buy…" heads off the "how do I invest?" comments.

> Start with one task you do every week. Do it with AI this week.

The takeaway, as an action they can do this week.

> Want a list to start with? Comment SHIFT and I'll send you five tasks you can hand to AI today.

The CTA. The keyword matches the core idea, and the DM is the next step.

## DM (SHIFT)

Five tasks, each one line, each something the viewer can do today: meeting notes, the
email you keep putting off, a long PDF, a messy spreadsheet, a hard conversation.

## What the notes held

- Every number came from a named source (Newsweek citing Bloomberg, CNBC/AP for the IPO).
- "$7.5B" was our maths (0.5% stake × $1.5T), so it is labelled as such.
- Shoot-day re-check: the market value after the first trading day.
- Caption ends "(Not investment advice.)".
