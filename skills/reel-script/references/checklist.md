# Checklist before showing a script

Go top to bottom. Each line is pass or fail. Fix every fail before you show the script.

## 1. Use

- [ ] A viewer can do, try or decide something after watching. Name it in one line.
- [ ] One core idea. Every paragraph serves it. Side tips moved to the DM.
- [ ] The DM resource exists in full and takes about 10 minutes to use. Or the reel ends on a real question.

## 2. Clarity for a stranger

- [ ] Hook passes `hooks.md` rules.
- [ ] One analogy, from daily life, in 2–3 sentences. Two analogies for one point: cut one.
- [ ] About 3 numbers or fewer, spoken in words ("sixty percent more", not "1.6×").
- [ ] No jargon the audience would not use (parameters, inference, fund, ARR, sovereign
      compute, model names without a 5-word "what it is").
- [ ] 110–150 words (about 2.5 words a second = 45–60 seconds).

## 3. AI-slop

Run the `no-ai-slop` skill if it is installed (github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop, MIT).
Then check the patterns we kept catching in our own drafts:

- [ ] No "not X, it's Y" / "don't ask X, ask Y" contrasts. State Y.
- [ ] No "here's the part people miss", "here's the thing", "what nobody tells you".
- [ ] No fragment kickers ("That's it. That's the whole trick.") and no fake-deep last line.
- [ ] No colon reveals ("The best part: it learns.").
- [ ] No unprovable claims ("faster than ever", "the best tool out there").
- [ ] No banned words: game changer, this is huge, leverage, unlock, supercharge, seamless.
- [ ] No em dashes in spoken copy or captions.
- [ ] Exception: the reviewer's own lines stay as written.

## 4. Honesty

- [ ] **Who can do it.** "This ran on my MacBook", not "anyone can run it". Hardware and
      price go in the DM. (Caught: "a model anyone can download, running on a laptop" needed a 16–32 GB Mac.)
- [ ] **Only fully true claims.** Drop "Wi-Fi off" if the cloud did part of the work.
- [ ] **Who got the money.** "He and his partners", never "Kutcher made 7.5 billion" when
      a fund with outside investors holds the stake.
- [ ] **Scale words match the data.** Traffic "flat for a year" is fine; "nobody uses it" is not.
- [ ] **Single sources are attributed.** One tester's result is "in his test". An anonymous or unverified post ("6 of 8 models are Chinese") stays out.
- [ ] **Conflicting sources** get wording true under both, noted in Source notes.
- [ ] **Fresh enough** for the shoot day, or the hook leads with the takeaway instead.
- [ ] **Our maths is labelled** in Source notes, with the sum.
- [ ] **Self-reported numbers** say so ("Perplexity says…"). Run rate is not profit.
- [ ] **Money topics** end the caption with "(Not investment advice.)" and use no "buy" language.
- [ ] **No rumours or allegations** about people (affairs, spying, crimes) and nothing the
      channel brief's house rules forbid.
- [ ] Facts that can change before the shoot are listed in Status as a re-check.

## 5. Rights and safety

- [ ] No AI image or video of another real person's face. Use only the presenter's own likeness, with their consent.
- [ ] No logos, title sequences or theme music from films or brands (Instagram mutes or
      removes them). Make your own title card.
- [ ] AI-made scenes are labelled as AI in the caption and with Instagram's AI label.
- [ ] Real posts, photos or clips of other people appear only as credited screenshots or short clips, unedited.
- [ ] Production note in Status: if the reel has AI scenes of the presenter, a still of the exact look gets their yes before any video is rendered.

## 6. Brand

- [ ] Brand voice skill run if the channel brief names one (Airtribe: `airtribe-voice`).
- [ ] House rules in the channel brief all pass.
